## English (en-US)

**App name (24/30):**
Şut ve Gol: Soccer Shots

**Short description (75/80):**
Swipe, bend and hit the ring! Unlock King Mode and win online soccer duels.

**Full description:**

Put your finger on the ball, swipe and shoot! In Şut ve Gol your swipe direction is your aim and your finger speed is your power; drag in an arc and the ball bends. Hit the ring inside the goal, rack up points and break your record.

Note: the in-game text is currently in Turkish. The controls are a single swipe, so you can play right away.

SWIPE, AIM, SCORE

• One-finger controls: direction, power and curve in a single move.
• Every hit is +30 points. A ball off the post drops to the ground and still counts if it lands on the ring.
• You have three hearts: a miss or a keeper save costs one and resets your streak.
• The camera switches between wide and close views, so every shot feels a little different.

KING MODE

• Five hits in a row unlock King Mode: a golden ball, sparkles and +60 points per hit for 10 seconds.
• Miss or run out of time and it bursts; start a new streak to bring it back.

FOUR PITCHES, FROM THE STREET TO THE STADIUM

• Street, Beach, Cage and Stadium: the scene changes as your score grows, each with its own lighting and sound.
• The first keeper comes on at 30 points and a second at 420; both move faster as your score rises.
• Bend your shots to find the gap.

ONLINE DUELS

• 90 seconds, a real rival, one winner. Your rival's pitch is on top and yours below, with live scores and streaks.
• If no one is online right then, you play against a real player's recorded run.
• Live matches change your duel rating and every match earns XP; when it ends you can ask for a rematch.

LEADERBOARDS AND ACCOUNT

• See where you stand on the daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards.
• Play right away as a guest. With an optional free account your record and stats are saved to the cloud and restored on a new device. Online duels and submitting scores to the leaderboard need an account.
• You can permanently delete your account from inside the app at any time.

A SAFE COMMUNITY

• Inappropriate usernames are filtered automatically.
• You can report or block a player in duels and on the leaderboard; reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

EASY TO LEARN

• A short first-run tutorial shows aim, curve and power step by step; you can skip it.
• "Nasıl oynanır" (How to play) in the pause menu opens it again any time.

All art and sounds are original to the game. Sound and vibration can be turned on or off in the pause menu. Free to play; ads appear only between plays, never while you shoot. No in-app purchases.

**Release notes (en-US, 222/500):**
Şut ve Gol is now on Android! Swipe to shoot, bend the ball and hit the ring. Land 5 in a row to unlock King Mode, play through four pitches from the street to the stadium and take on real rivals in 90-second online duels.

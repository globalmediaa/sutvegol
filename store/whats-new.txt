EN: Şut ve Gol build 10
• First-run tutorial for new players: a live finger animation shows aim, curve and power, then the ring, the keeper, hearts and stages.
• Your first shot is guided. Tap "Geç" to skip it; it won't show again.
• "Nasıl oynanır" (How to play) added to the pause menu.
